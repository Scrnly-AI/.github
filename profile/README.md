## What we do

We attack AI systems, applications and infrastructure the way a real adversary would. Then we help your engineers fix what we find and re-test every fix before we close it.

### 🧠 AI systems

| Service | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [AI Model & LLM Penetration Testing](https://scrnly.com/services/ai-model-pentesting) | Prompt injection, jailbreaks, data exfiltration and guardrail bypass, with bypass rates measured per technique |
| [Agentic AI Penetration Testing](https://scrnly.com/services/agentic-ai-pentesting) | Chained tool calls, delegation and memory abuse that lead to unauthorized actions, mapped to the OWASP LLM Top 10 |
| [AI Security & MLSecOps](https://scrnly.com/services/ai-security-mlsecops) | AI inventory, threat modelling, hardening and a maintained AI risk register |
